# Phase 123 Factor Explainability, Feature Attribution, Factor Interpretation and Research Report Layer

This PR implements Phase 123 of the Advanced Feature/Factor Engine band.

**Core Additions:**
* **Phase 123 Models (`usa_signal_bot/feature_engine/factor_explainability/phase123_models.py`)**: Data models for FactorValidationIngestionResult, ExplainabilityInputBundle, FeatureAttributionSpec, FeatureAttributionResult, FactorContributionProfile, FactorInterpretationSummary, FactorExplanationReport, ResearchReportSection, ResearchReportDocument, ReportQaRuleResult, ExplainabilityContext, ExplainabilityFullReview. Includes safe validation logic enforcing strict non-execution safety.
* **Factor Validation Ingestion (`factor_validation_ingestion.py`)**: Read-only ingestion of Phase 122 review outputs ensuring `research_data_only=True` and blocking execution permissions.
* **Artifact Loader (`factor_report_artifact_loader.py`)**: CSV and JSON artifact loading with traversal protection and forbidden column checking to catch and block `["buy", "sell", "entry", "exit", "order", "portfolio_weight", "target_weight", "allocation"]`.
* **Attribution Specs & Engine (`attribution_specs.py`, `feature_attribution_engine.py`)**: Generates mathematical heuristics (like deterministic proxy correlations) for feature contributions to factors safely, normalizing outputs and generating directions without reliance on ML logic like SHAP/LIME or suggesting trade execution.
* **Contribution & Interpretation Builders (`factor_contribution_analyzer.py`, `factor_interpretation_builder.py`, `diagnostics_interpretation.py`, `drift_interpretation.py`, `lineage_quality_interpretation.py`)**: Constructs text summaries based on metadata profiles. Employs a strict language safety policy forbidding any investment advice or execution tone.
* **Research Reports & Renderers (`research_report_sections.py`, `research_report_document.py`, `markdown_report_renderer.py`, `json_report_renderer.py`)**: Assembles explanations and outputs them as read-only documents (Markdown/JSON) protected by robust SHA-256 hashes and explicit disclaimer sections.
* **QA & Safety Verification (`report_qa_validator.py`, `explainability_safety_validator.py`)**: Scans texts for matched terms like "buy signal", "sell signal", "garanti kâr", "kesin al", validating compliance before allowing the bundle to transition to Phase 124 readied statuses.
* **Storage & Review Generation (`explainability_store.py`, `explainability_report.py`, `explainability_validation.py`, `explainability_reporting.py`)**: Persists outputs and guarantees payload validation on saving / fetching.
* **Docs, Health, Configuration, Enums & Exceptions**: Updated configurations and CLI wrappers to align with Phase 123 goals.

**Constraints:**
* No broker integrations.
* No live/paper orders.
* No dashboards or web scraping.
* No network default usages.
* No Telegram real sends.
