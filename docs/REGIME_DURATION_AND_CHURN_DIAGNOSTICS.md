# Regime Duration and Churn Diagnostics

Evaluates the temporal stability and volatility of regime labels.

- **Duration Profile:** Min, max, and median lengths of consecutive regimes.
- **Label Switch Count:** Total number of label changes.
- **Switch Rate & Churn Level:** High switch rates trigger high or extreme churn categorizations.
- **Low Confidence Count:** Instances where the regime confidence falls below acceptable thresholds.

Note: Churn diagnostics are for data quality monitoring and model assessment. They DO NOT serve as trade alerts.
