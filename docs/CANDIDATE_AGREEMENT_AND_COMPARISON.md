# Candidate Agreement and Comparison

Diagnostic artifacts that evaluate how candidate models perform individually vs together:
- **Pairwise Agreement / Disagreement:** Correlation of predictions between two models.
- **Ensemble Agreement with Candidate:** How closely the ensemble matches an individual candidate.
- **Score Distance:** Delta between probability scores.
- **Ensemble vs Best/Median/Top-Ranked Group Comparison:** Research comparisons of the ensemble vs individual models.

Comparisons are strictly research data and do NOT represent trade recommendations.
