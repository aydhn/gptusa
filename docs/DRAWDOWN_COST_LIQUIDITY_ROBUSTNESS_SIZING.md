# Extended Sizing Prototypes

## Drawdown Adjusted Sizing
Applies penalties for candidates demonstrating significant maximum drawdown history.

## Cost Aware Sizing
Adjusts sizing downward for candidates displaying elevated estimated slip or transaction costs.

## Liquidity Aware Sizing
Scales down candidate exposure in proportion to liquidity deficiency (e.g., low ADV or high bid-ask spread).

## Robustness Adjusted Sizing
Penalizes size relative to structural model stability/robustness proxies.

All methods explicitly confirm they are NOT investment advice and produce NO capital allocation instructions.
