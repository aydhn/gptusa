# Phase 157 Summary

Phase 157 acts as the closing stage of the Portfolio Risk, Construction, and Optimization band.
It creates:
- The Risk Reporting Context
- Sandbox Exposure Governance
- Portfolio Risk Summary and concentration/diversification metrics
- Artifact Lineage and Compliance Audit
- Safety Boundaries and Phase 158 Handoff Packaging

It explicitly does not trade, output target weights, or deploy capital.
