# Provider Limitations

* **Free Provider Constraints**: Free services like YFinance are subject to rate-limiting and temporary outages. They are not official exchange feeds.
* **Cache Staleness**: Cached data becomes stale if a fetch isn't successfully completed recently.
* **Not Investment Advice**: Provider scores purely reflect data structure/freshness and are not indicators of trading success or investment advice.
* **No Broken APIs**: No real or demo broker execution will occur based on the results of the provider fetches.
