# Market Behavior Profiles

A behavior profile aggregates artifacts into thematic insights. The following behavior profiles are supported:

- **Transition Behavior Profile**: Analyzes dominant regime transitions.
- **Persistence Behavior Profile**: Summarizes average and median run lengths.
- **Duration / Churn Behavior Profile**: Identifies sequence stability based on chunk frequencies.
- **Stability Behavior Profile**: Interprets regime consistency across rolling windows.
- **Cross-Symbol Behavior Profile**: Provides high-level aggregated trends across multiple symbols.
- **Data Quality Behavior Profile**: Tracks any missing inputs or schema issues from Phase 129.

### Important Disclaimer
Market Behavior Profiles are research metadata. They do not constitute **trade signals** and they do not dictate **portfolio weights**.
