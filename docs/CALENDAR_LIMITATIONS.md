# Calendar and Guard Limitations

- Local/manual calendar is **not** an official exchange guarantee.
- Users must update holiday/early close files themselves or trust defaults.
- Provider corporate action metadata from YFinance may be delayed or missing.
- Split/dividend detection relies on price gaps and is strictly a heuristic.
- Gap anomalies might just be market volatility, not a corporate action.
- The absence of adjusted data is not always fatal but warrants warnings.
- **Calendar/Session/Corporate Action Guards do NOT act as investment advice.**
- We do not integrate with live broker connections or demo orders.
- No web scraping is performed.
