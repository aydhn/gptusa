# Portfolio Diversification and Concentration Diagnostics

## Diversification Mechanics
Calculates the level of exposure dispersion:
- **Effective Name Count:** Interprets fractional boundaries into theoretical units.
- **Herfindahl Index:** Uses the sum of squares formula to track internal competition for weight.

## Concentration Diagnostics
Evaluates upper-bound exposures:
- **Top-N Concentration:** Identifies aggregated allocation sizes within the densest segments (typically Top 5).
- **Max Sandbox Weight:** Ensures compliance against absolute ceiling restrictions per item.

## Application
The resulting numbers serve exclusively as meta-diagnostics and are completely disconnected from realistic integration pipelines.
