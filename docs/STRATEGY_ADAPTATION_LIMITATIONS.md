# Strategy Adaptation Limitations

1. **Heuristic Nature**: Strategy adaptation is heuristic and relies entirely on backward-looking metrics.
2. **Classification Failure**: Regime classification may fail, lag, or misidentify market phases.
3. **No Certainty**: Ensemble consensus is NOT a guarantee of success.
4. **Not Live Trading Approval**: Gate PASS does not grant live trading approval.
5. **No Orders**: Candidate suppression is metadata only; no broker orders are issued.
6. **No Advice**: This system is strictly local, experimental, and is **NOT investment advice**.
