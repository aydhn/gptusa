# Brier Decomposition

Calculates the Brier score and its decomposition components.

## Components
- **Brier Score:** Overall mean squared error of probabilistic forecasts.
- **Reliability:** How well forecasts align with observed frequencies.
- **Resolution:** Ability to distinguish between different event frequencies.
- **Uncertainty:** Inherent variability of the outcomes.

*Note: The Brier metric is a statistical diagnostic, NOT a PnL, trading, or backtest metric.*
