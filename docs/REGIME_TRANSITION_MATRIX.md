# Regime Transition Matrix

Calculates transition behavior across consecutive periods for heuristic or rolling regime labels.

- **Transition Counts:** Raw number of label-to-label switches.
- **Transition Probabilities:** Normalized transitions from a specific regime.
- **Self-Transition Rate:** Frequency of staying in the same regime.
- **Switch Rate:** Frequency of switching regimes.
- **Dominant Transition:** The most frequent non-self transition.
- **Entropy/Concentration Proxy:** A metric for transition predictability.

Note: Transition matrices are metadata summaries. They DO NOT serve as trade signals.
