# Regime Stability Diagnostics

Calculates a normalized score (0-100) representing the robustness of the regime labeling over time.

- **Stability Score:** Aggregated assessment. High scores indicate stable, clear regimes.
- **Sequence Fragmentation:** Measures short, rapid regime shifts.
- **Dominant Label Concentration:** Focuses on the frequency of the most common regime.

Note: Stability diagnostics guide model tuning and do not imply strategy activation or trading decisions.
