# Board Dossier Limitations

- **Local Metadata**: This dossier is exclusively a local JSON structure. It has no integration with live markets or APIs.
- **Not an Approval**: Receiving a `VALIDATED_NON_EXECUTION` status does not mean you have approval to trade.
- **No Broker API**: The bot cannot send live or demo orders.
- **No Paper Mutations**: Nothing in Phase 95 mutates the paper mode runtime.
- **Not Investment Advice**: The metrics and decisions reflect internal workflow rules, not financial advice.
